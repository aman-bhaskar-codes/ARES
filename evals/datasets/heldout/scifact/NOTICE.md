# SciFact attribution and rights

Source: Allen Institute for AI, SciFact, https://github.com/allenai/scifact
Paper: Wadden et al., **Fact or Fiction: Verifying Scientific Claims**, EMNLP 2020, https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.14974
Original rights notice: https://github.com/allenai/scifact/blob/master/LICENSE.md

Claims and evidence annotations are licensed under **CC BY 4.0**: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Corpus abstracts are from Semantic Scholar S2ORC and licensed under **ODC-By 1.0**: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/by/1-0/

This subset preserves source claim/document identifiers and original annotation labels. ARES maps SUPPORT to supported and CONTRADICT without supporting evidence to abstention for its own evaluation vocabulary. Retrieval cases use relevant abstracts and a bounded distractor pool; relevance grade 3 is assigned to annotated evidence documents. No original scientific annotations were edited to improve ARES scores. The archive SHA-256 and selection rule are in manifest.json.
